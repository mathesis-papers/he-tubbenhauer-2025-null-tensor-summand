# Quantitative null tensor summands for He–Tubbenhauer’s Conjecture 4.2 (arXiv:2508.04054v1, p. 17): extending their 2025 theorem

**Authors:** Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

Extending the right-idempotent-generated case proved by He and Tubbenhauer in 2025, we prove their Conjecture 4.2 in Tensor powers of representations of (diagram) monoids, arXiv:2508.04054v1, p. 17: a finite-dimensional representation of a finite semigroup, with every element acting singularly, has a one-dimensional zero-action direct summand in a positive tensor power, and its indecomposable-summand count is asymptotic to the tensor dimension. The proof works over every field and bounds the deficit by twice the sum of the action-rank powers. A classical multiplicity identity gives the exact maximal null summand. The same argument proves the related monoid Conjecture 3A.2 when the unit-group order is invertible in the field. A two-element right-zero example corrects the literal finite-exponent equality printed in Proposition 4.4 to an asymptotic statement. Explicit Jordan multiplicities and the recurrence of the published fusion matrix are also given.
